I thought Napoli at Kirkwood was okay. It's nice that there's an outdoor patio, but it's right along an extremely loud and busy street, so it doesn't have the best ambience. They don't make their own toasted ravioli, which I expected because they make some of their own pasta. Since it's a bigger location it might be good for a big group, but I'd rather go to Louie or Pizzeria da Gloria or some other Italian places, personally.
