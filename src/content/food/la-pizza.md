La Pizza is the only place in St. Louis (as far as I can tell) where you can get an actual authentic New York-style pizza. It's a small shop, mostly for takeout with a few small tables.

It has the atmosphere of a friendly neighborhood joint and the vibes are truly 10/10. We showed up and they were busy with take out orders all filled up for the rest of the night, and they managed to fit us in and make us a fresh pizza and wings. We got a sausage and pepperoni pizza.

As much as I liked the place, I thought the pizza was just okay. One thing is I'm not a fan of strips of sausage, which I know is just the way it's often done with NY-style, so I need to learn my lesson and just get pepperoni. The midwest in me just always wants the bulk sausage on my pizza. The crust didn't have much flavor to me. I wouldn't go out of my way to try La Pizza again, but I thought it was an overall decent pizza. Just not amazing.
