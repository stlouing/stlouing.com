Park Avenue is a local coffee chain that has locations in Lafayette Square, [[the-hill|The Hill]], [[midtown|Midtown]], and [[downtown|Downtown]]. They also make one of the better gooey butter cakes around town.

The coffee from Park Avenue is the best - I order it as my regular, daily coffee. It is much less acidic than most other coffee roasters, and they have some nice, subtle flavored coffees like cinnamon hazelnut and southern pecan.
