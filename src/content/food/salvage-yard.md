I have yet to try the Chicago deep dish at Salvage Yard, but I have had their thin crust St. Louis-style pizza, and I thought it was a really good one. I plan to go back and get some pictures and try the deep dish.

They also have something called the B.O.B - "The Best of Both is a signature cooking style on Chicago pizzas! It starts with homemade crust, St. Louis smooth and slightly sweet sauce and a St. Louis cheese blend!". I'm not sure I'm sold on that, I haven't been a fan of Provel on thick crust pizzas, but I will definitely try it at some point.
