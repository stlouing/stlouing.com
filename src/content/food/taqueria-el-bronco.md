I've been on the lookout for a particular type of burrito - grilled steak, with lettuce, tomato, sour cream, queso fresco, and refried beans, on a griddled tortilla. Taqueria El Bronco is one of the first spots I tried to find this, but it didn't hit for me so I haven't been back since. I'm sure there's something else on the menu that is much better, so I'm planning to go back and give it another shot at some point.
