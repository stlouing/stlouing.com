I'm fortunate enough to have tried Mom's Deli at its original, iconic location before it got relocated to the Hampton strip. Mom's Deli is a classic St. Louis institution, and I understand a lot of people have been going here for their whole life and have strong feelings about it.

Personally, Mom's Deli isn't really the spot for me - I'd rather go to [[Blues City Deli|blues-city-deli]] or [[The Gramophone|the-gramophone]] for a more upscale sandwich experience, but an everyday sandwich it's alright.
