Midtown is an area between [[downtown|Downtown]] and [[forest-park|Forest Park]] that is seeing a lot of growth right now. One of the biggest draws of the area is the City Foundry, a food hall and market built into a restored vacant foundry building, and The Cortex Innovation Community, a tech hub in St. Louis providing a coworking and office space for startups and businesses.

In the Grand Center area, there are a ton of museums, theatres, and art galleries, such as the Fabulous Fox theatre, the Sun Theatre and Powell Hall.

I'm hopeful about the innovation projects that are being implemented to help stimulate the economy and add more energy and vibrancy to the city. Between the office spaces, the theatres, the food halls and other dining, there's a lot going on in Midtown.

### Impressions

The Cortex area of Midtown (technically in Central West End) does not feel very walkable, and the food hall is surrounded on all sides by highways, so it's not as easy to get to as other food halls that are located right in a downtown walkable area like in Nashville. There's a large Ikea and some large office buildings and a lot of space dedicated to parking lots in that area. Hopefully some of the projects like the Greenway will make this area more walkable and bikable.
