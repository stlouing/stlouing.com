Midtown is an area between [[downtown|Downtown]] and [[forest-park|Forest Park]] that is seeing a lot of growth right now. One of the biggest draws of the area is the City Foundry, a food hall and market built into a restored vacant foundry building, and The Cortex Innovation Community, a tech hub in St. Louis providing a coworking and office space for startups and businesses.

Just north is [[covenant-blu-grand-center|Grand Center]], the theatre and arts district.

I'm hopeful about the innovation projects that are being implemented to help stimulate the economy and add more energy and vibrancy to the city. Between the office spaces, the food halls and other dining, there's a lot going on in Midtown.

There is also [Midtown Alley](https://www.midtownalley.com/), consisting of some businesses and breweries along Locust St.
