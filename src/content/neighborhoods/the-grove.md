The Grove is the LGBTQ neighborhood in St. Louis, and is home to a strip of local restaurants and bars, located in the Forest Park Southeast neighborhood.

It's also the only district in St. Louis that has a cute little [neon entry sign](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/16/Grove_marker.jpg). I'm a big fan of this sign, as it reminds me of all the [signs around neighborhoods in San Diego](https://www.socalregion.com/images-of-socal/signs-of-san-diego/). I genuinely hope more neighborhood strips in St. Louis add them, though I'm not sure where else would be a contender. Maybe Cherokee St., DeMun, or the Delmar Loop.

The Grove was one of the first places in St. Louis I was introduced to, as I had met up with some coworkers at The Gramophone, a popular sandwich shop. I've also been to Urban Chestnut, which is a brewery with a great vibe. (I went to both of these before I started paying attention to the food enough, so I'll have to revisit before I make entries for those spots in the [Food map](/food/)).

A few of my favorite restaurants in the city are in the Grove. I think [[sultan|Sultan]] is one of the best Middle Eastern restaurants around. [[grace-meat-and-three|Grace Meat and Three]] was a good fried chicken spot, and Pie Guy was the best New York-style pizza I've had, but unfortunately they both closed.
