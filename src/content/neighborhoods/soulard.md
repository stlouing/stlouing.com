title: Soulard
created: 2026-07-05
updated: 2026-11-07
---

Soulard is known as the bar and nightlife part of town. It's the oldest residential neighborhood in St. Louis, and has kept much of its original character intact. There are many small, winding streets with bars, restaurants, clubs, cafes, and live music dotted throughout.

Unfortunately, a large highway runs right through the neighborhood, as the whole area was seen as poor and obsolete and was planned for demolition after World War II, to be replaced with the more modern suburban homes and car-centric layouts we see almost everywhere else. Fortunately, enough people who cared prevented this from happening and Soulard got placed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1972, but the highway that runs through still prevents it from reaching its full potential.
