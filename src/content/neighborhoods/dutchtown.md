Dutchtown is one of the most densely populated areas of St. Louis city.

The area was originally populated by German immigrants in late nineteenth century, which is where the name Dutchtown (Deutschtown) originated. Currently, it is one of the more diverse neighborhoods in South City, with a significant black and Hispanic population.

[Dutchtown Proud](https://dutchtownstl.org/2020/06/dutchtown-proud/) is the motto of this neighborhood, and you'll see "Dutchtown Proud" signs in yards all around. A lot of people clearly take pride in the neighborhood are putting in a lot of work to make Dutchtown a good place to live, work, and visit.
