Chesterfield is a large, sprawling suburb. It has a lot of chain restaurants, shopping outlets, Topgolf, and large multi-lane highways. One major highlight of this suburb is The District, a large entertainment district with a concert hall (The Factory) a food hall, restaurants and breweries. This acts as a walkable downtown to an otherwise very car-centric suburb.
