Chesterfield is the epitome of suburban sprawl. Chain restaurants, shopping outlets, Topgolf, and large multi-lane highways. One major highlight of this suburb is The District, a large entertainment district with a concert hall (The Factory) a food hall, restaurants and breweries. This gives some semblance of a walkable downtown to an otherwise very car-centric suburb.
