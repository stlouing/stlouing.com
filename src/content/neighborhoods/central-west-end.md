Central West End is an upscale urban neighborhood in St. Louis city. It sits right along Forest Park and has a walkable downtown vibe. Of all the neighborhoods I've explored, Central West End has the most vibrant energy with people walking around and sitting at outdoor cafes and restaurants, or visiting the various book stores and boutique stores.

It's also home to Up-Down Arcade, an arcade full of retro games, craft beers, and a pizza window.
