Webster Groves is a charming, upscale suburb with two main walkable strips. On the west end, you have [[telva-at-the-ridge|Telva at the Ridge]], a Balkan-inspired cafe from the owners of Balkan Treat Box, and Olive + Oak, an intimate and upscale restaurant and cocktail bar. On the east end, you have Balkan Treat Box on a strip with multiple small, locally owned bookstores, some cafes, and Webster University.
