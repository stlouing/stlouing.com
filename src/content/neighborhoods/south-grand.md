South Grand is a business district just south of Tower Grove Park with six blocks of international dining options and small, independent businesses.

It sits on the line between [[tower-grove-south|Tower Grove South]] and Tower Grove East. Along this strip you'll find a concentrated variety of restaurants, from Persian, Lebanese, and Turkish to pizza, hot dogs, and cafes.

There are also some small, independent stores such as Dunaway Books, Apotheosis Comics, and Circa: Now! Records, which all together make this a good area for walking, shopping and eating.
