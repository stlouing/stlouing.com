Southampton is a charming neighborhood close to many bars and restaurants all along Hampton Ave, such as Mom's Deli, J.J. Twig's, Salvage Yard, and Salt & Smoke BBQ. The area is affectionately known as "SOHA". There are many styles of brick-built homes in Southampton have a lot of character. It's a quiet neighborhood close to Francis Park in St. Louis Hills, and also not too far from Tower Grove Park.
