## My Ratings

Here's my subjective rated list of the places I've tried.

1. [[farottos|Farotto's]] - A pizza like no other in town, with extra-extra-thin buttery crust, a slightly sweet sauce, and quality ingredients. Just don't get Jimmy's favorite.
1. Pirrone's - A rectangular pizza with a crispy, buttery crust.
1. Faraci's - Similar to Pirrone's in size and shape, with a more bready, yeasty crust.
1. Salvage Yard
1. Firehouse
1. [[failonis|Failoni's]] - A brick-oven pizza with the most cracker-like like crust
1. Imo's - By far the most famous and infamous pizza in town, Imo's is THE St. Louis pizza chain and the only STL-style many people have ever tried. Imo's has a mass-produced flavor to it, unlike the homestyle vibes of a lot of other spots. The quality also varies a lot from location to location.
1. Nick & Elena's - I'm sorry. I know people love this place, and the old-school vibes are top-notch, but I didn't like the pizza (specifically the sauce).
1. Cecil Whittaker's - Bottom of the barrel for St. Louis-style pizza. It's like an Imo's clone with lower quality ingredients. We ordered it once and it was a DNF experience.
